# Telling me what you think

If you are trying repetita out for me, this page is the whole of what I need
from you. There is no form, no survey and nothing to install.

## The button

Top right, on every screen: **Say something**.

```
   🇮🇹 ▾   repetita    Study  Design  Create  Manage    3 owed  7 / 30   [ Say something ]
```

It opens a box. Write whatever you were thinking, in whatever language you think
in — Polish, English, half of each. Press **Send** (or <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>⏎</kbd>).

There is no wrong thing to write in it. The useful ones are usually the small
ones:

* *"I did not understand why this word came back a third time today."*
* *"I wanted to fix the typo but could not find where."*
* *"I stopped after ten because it got boring."*
* *"This was the first time it felt like it was working."*

**"I got bored and closed it" is a real answer** and one of the most useful ones.
A prototype nobody wants to open twice has a problem no bug report will find.

### What it records with your words

The moment, which screen you were on, which course, and the version of the app.
That is the difference between a note I can act on and one I cannot: *"this is
confusing"* means one thing on the Study screen and something else entirely on
Manage, and neither of us will remember which in three days.

It records nothing else. Not what you answered, not what you got wrong.

## Where it goes

Straight to a file, inside the repetita folder on your own computer:

```
repetita/feedback/<your name>/2026-09-14-193211-manage.md
```

You can open it, read it, and delete it if you change your mind. Nothing is sent
anywhere at the moment you press Send — it reaches me the next time your copy
syncs, along with anything you fixed in the material.

## Fixing something yourself

If you spot a wrong translation or a typo, you do not have to write to me about
it — fix it. **Manage** → click the exercise → *Edit this exercise*. Your fix
travels back the same way your notes do.

There is a separate button for a *broken exercise*, under every card while you
are studying: **Something's wrong**. Use that one when the exercise itself is at
fault — the answer is wrong, the hint gives it away, the question is ambiguous.
It suspends the card immediately, so it stops costing you reviews while it waits
to be fixed. "Say something" is for everything else.

## What I will not see

Your schedule and your answers stay on your computer. The database they live in
is never shared and never leaves your machine, and there is no account and no
server — each copy of repetita is entirely its own.

If you want to stop, delete the folder. That is all of it.
