# reference/

Source material used to author course content — **never course content itself,
and never redistributed from here.**

Everything under this directory is gitignored, and not because of its size.

## Why nothing here is committed

A course in `courses/` is CC BY-SA 4.0. Material licensed **NonCommercial** is
one-way incompatible with that: CC BY-SA permits commercial use, an NC licence
forbids it, so NC material cannot be relicensed into a BY-SA course no matter how
it is transformed.

That is not a reason to avoid such a source. Reading a textbook and then writing
your own exercises is exactly what a textbook is for. It is a reason to keep the
extracted material on one side of a line that a later contributor cannot cross by
accident — including a later contributor who notices the files are only a few
hundred kilobytes and assumes size was the objection.

`livro/LICENCA.md` records where each licence was found, verbatim, and what it
permits. Read it before using anything here.

## Using it correctly

Take the **structure** — which grammar point comes before which, which verbs a
beginner meets first, which tenses a lesson introduces. Sequencing decisions are
facts about teaching a language, not expression owned by a publisher.

Do not take the sentences. Write your own, about your own life, which makes
better exercises anyway: an exercise about the roda you actually train at is
worth more than one about a fictional student in a fictional cafeteria.
